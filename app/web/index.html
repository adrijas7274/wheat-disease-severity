      Decision support only. This app does not replace an agriculture expert. The severity levels are placeholders. The spray advice is copied from the official CIBRC list of registered uses and has not yet been checked by an agriculture expert; per-acre and per-tank amounts are converted from the official figures.

      Everything runs on this phone. Photos are not uploaded. Saved scans stay on this phone.
