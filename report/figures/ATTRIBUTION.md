# Figure attribution

## Figures derived from PlantSeg

These figures contain images from PlantSeg v7, and some also show its masks:

- `crop_selection/`
- `tag_review/`, including `tag_review/chunks/`
- `wheat_sample_overlays.png`
- `recheck/same_leaf_check.png` (SAM 2 leaf outlines and click markers drawn on)
- `leaf_masks/tuning_rules.png` (SAM 2 leaf outlines and click markers drawn on)
- `leaf_masks/leaf_rule_review_sheet.png` (SAM 2 leaf outlines, click markers and review verdicts drawn on)

**Changes made:** images were selected, resized and arranged into grids. Some
have PlantSeg masks drawn over them, and some have added text labels or index
numbers. These figures were made for this project and are not part of the
original dataset. The PlantSeg authors have not reviewed or endorsed them.

**Dataset:** Wei, T., Chen, Z., Yu, X., Chapman, S., Melloy, P., & Huang, Z.
(2025). *A Large-Scale In-the-wild Dataset for Plant Disease Segmentation*
[Data set, v7]. Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17719108>

**Paper:** Wei, T., Chen, Z., Yu, X., Chapman, S., Melloy, P., & Huang, Z.
(2024). *PlantSeg: A Large-Scale In-the-wild Dataset for Plant Disease
Segmentation*. arXiv:2409.04038. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04038>

**Licence:** the dataset is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>). These figures are shared
for non-commercial academic use, under the same licence.

## Figures derived from the external wheat sets (CC BY 4.0)

- `phase7/overlays/` and `phase7_final/overlays/`: photos from the **Mendeley Wheat Leaf Dataset**, Getachew, H.
  (2021), <https://doi.org/10.17632/wgd66f8n6h.1>, CC BY 4.0.
- `demo/` (`demo.gif`, `retake.png`): screenshots of the demo showing JIC photos (`YellowRust3034.png`, and
  `BrownRust2453.png` with added blur), same JIC attribution and licence as below.
- `phase7_jic/overlays/`: photos from the **JIC wheat disease images**, Long, M. C., Hartley, M., Morris, R. J. &
  Brown, J. K. M. (2023), Zenodo, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7573133>, CC BY 4.0.

**Changes made:** downscaled to a 2048 px long side and resized, with the predicted leaf outline, lesion pixels
and click drawn on. Licence: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. The authors have not reviewed or
endorsed these figures.

## Figures not derived from PlantSeg

`area_diagrams/` contains synthetic reference diagrams generated by this project
(`src/severity/area_diagrams.py`). They contain no PlantSeg images.
