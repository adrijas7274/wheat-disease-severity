# Treatment advice: sources (UNVERIFIED until an agriculture expert checks it)

- **cibrc_mup**: Major Uses of Pesticides (Registered under the Insecticides Act, 1968) - Fungicides (upto 30/09/2026). Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC), Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. As on 2026-09-30, accessed 2026-11-23.
  https://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/files/Fungicide%20MUP%20%20as%20on%2030.09.2026.pdf
  The document says it is compiled 'for guidance and not for legal purposes'. Doses are per hectare.
- **cibrc_banned**: List of pesticides which are banned, refused registration and restricted in use (updated on 31.10.2026). CIB&RC, Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage. As on 2026-10-31, accessed 2026-11-23.
  https://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/files/list_of_pesticides_which_are_banned_refused_registration_and_restricted_in_use%20%2831.10.26%29.pdf
  Mancozeb is on the restricted list (banned on guava, jowar and tapioca, S.O. 4294(E) of 03.10.2023). Restricted products are excluded here, so mancozeb (registered on wheat for brown and black rust) is left out. None of the products kept below is on the list.
- **ppqs_yr_advisory**: Advisory on yellow rust of wheat (Puccinia striiformis f.sp. tritici) to major wheat growing areas. Plant Protection Adviser, Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage, Faridabad. As on 2022-03 (scanned letter; day not legible), accessed 2026-11-23.
  https://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/files/advisroy_yellow_rust.pdf
  Says 'Fungicides must be used as per label claim approved by the Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee' and lists IPM practices; its annexure repeats the CIBRC propiconazole 25% EC row (125 g a.i., 500gm, 750 L, 30 days).
